Be in the conversation. Trilsy writes the notes.

Trilsy records your microphone and your Mac's sound together, transcribes it, and writes notes you can edit.

Apple silicon, macOS 15 or later

Trilsy records whatever your Mac plays, from the apps you already use.

App names and marks belong to their owners; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.
