B8, topic 1: what lives on your Mac and what lives on Trilsy's servers. Today everything is on the Mac; the paid MCP, integrations and "keeping notes for search" all hang on this

1. Local-first, hosted only for accounts and connectionsrecommended

Audio, transcript and notes never leave the Mac. Paid plan unlocks a local MCP and app connections.

2. Notes kept on Trilsy's servers for search

The notes (and perhaps transcripts) sync to an account so they can be searched anywhere and reached by any assistant.

3. Everything local, even the paid parts

A licence key unlocks features; connections and the MCP run on the Mac using your own sign-ins.
