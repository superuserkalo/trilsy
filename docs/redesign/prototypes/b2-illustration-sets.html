Notionists recommended

By
Zoish (heyzoish.gumroad.com/l/notionists), served as an avatar style by DiceBear
Licence
CC0 1.0, as stated on dicebear.com/styles/notionists (read 2026-10-13). Commercial use and modification: allowed under CC0. Attribution: not required (the page credits the creator as a courtesy).
Poses
Busts only: 25 bodies, 64 hair styles, 13 brows, 30 lips, 20 noses, 11 glasses, 12 beards, and 10 hand gestures (point, ok, wave, hand, hand on phone, long arms). Over a million combinations, so the faces vary a great deal and every one has an expression.
Files
SVG from a URL (api.dicebear.com/9.x/notionists/svg?seed=&gesture=), black and white, transparent. About 11 to 22 KB each.
Motion
Each SVG is split into 12 to 13 groups (body, face, hair, glasses, hand), so Motion can move a hand, tilt a head or swap lips between two variants. Static files should be saved into the repo rather than called live.
Gaps
No seated or standing full bodies, no table, no lectern; the table is our own line. Hands exist for ten gestures only, so a hand at the chin or on a laptop is not available. Dicebear's own code is MIT; the CC0 grant is for the artwork.

Open Peeps

By
Pablo Stanley (openpeeps.com)
Licence
CC0, "Free for commercial and personal use under CC0 License" (openpeeps.com, read 2026-10-13). Commercial use and modification allowed; no attribution required.
Poses
93 ready-made figures on the site: 49 busts (some with arms: pointing, shrugging, holding a mug, a laptop, a phone, arms crossed), 14 sitting, 30 standing. The site also advertises a mix-and-match kit (Sketch, Figma, XD; "584,688 combinations") that I could not open without the Gumroad download.
Files
SVG and PNG, black and white, 5 to 10 big filled paths each.
Motion
Each figure is one drawing, not layers: Motion can nudge, scale or tilt the whole person and draw bubbles around them, but cannot change a mouth or move a hand. Separate heads and arms would need the mix-and-match kit.
Gaps
The "sitting" figures are on the floor or crouching, not on a chair at a table (one is in a wheelchair). The lecture presenter is a standing figure with no lectern. The line is heavy and the clothing mostly solid black, which is the closest thing in this set to the "thick" fault the owner named.

Open Doodles

By
Pablo Stanley (opendoodles.com)
Licence
CC0 1.0 Public Domain badge; "Free for Commercial and Personal Use"; "No need to credit, license, or anything" (opendoodles.com, read 2026-10-13). The page does not mention modification, though CC0 permits it.
Poses
33 illustrations: relaxed, loose whole-body scenes (sitting on a chair, sitting on a sofa, reading, lying down, coffee, dancing, a dog).
Files
SVG and PNG, some GIF. Black ink with one pink accent in a separate "Accent" group, so the accent can be swapped for ours with one fill change. 150+ paths each.
Motion
Two groups (Ink, Accent) only; the whole figure can move, nothing inside it.
Gaps
Does not cover the brief. No one is talking, presenting or interviewing and there is no table scene, so it cannot do the hero's three settings. It would suit an empty state or a use-case page at best. Shown as the closest poses it has, with nothing contrived.
