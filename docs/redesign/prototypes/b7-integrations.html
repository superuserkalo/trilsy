Pattern: all but Superwhisper now offer an MCP server or connector, always framed as "use your meeting notes in the AI you already use", and the real integration lists are CRM, Slack, Notion and the calendar. Trilsy today has none of these. It saves plain JSON and files on your Mac and exports Markdown, text and SRT, so anything below is Coming soon.

Set 1: Your notes, where you writerecommended

Business and students. Files first: nothing to sign in to, true to a local app.

Obsidianfolder of MarkdownNotionimportApple NotesGoogle Docs

One "Send to" action that writes the Markdown note (and SRT or text) to a chosen folder or opens it in the app. Needs only the exports that exist; no accounts, no network. Students use Obsidian and Notion for study notes; teams use Notion and Docs.

Cost: it is a share action, not a sync. Each app needs its own check.

Set 2: Your AI assistantsrecommended

Business and students. The strongest signal in the research.

ClaudeChatGPTCursorany MCP client

A local MCP server (below) so an assistant can search and read your recordings on your Mac. It fits a local-first app, where the data never has to leave the machine, and matches what four of five competitors lead with.

Cost: the app has no server today, so this is new work, and "ChatGPT" depends on its MCP support being available to you.

Set 3: Work tools

Business first.

Google Calendartitle recordingsSlackpost a summaryNotion

Name a recording from the calendar event, and share a note to a channel. These are the most used in the research, and each needs sign-in and a network call, which the app does not have.

Cost: the largest build, accounts and tokens, and the point where "no server, no account" stops being true. CRM sync is deliberately not on the list.

A proposed MCP surfaceproposal

A local server the app runs, over stdio, read-only to start. Mirrors what is already on disk.

  • search_recordings text and date range, returns ids, titles, snippets
  • get_notes the overview and sections for one recording
  • get_transcript the full transcript with timestamps and the mic or system label
  • list_recordings recent first, with duration
  • Resources: each recording as a URI the assistant can attach

Not included: start or stop recording (consent and safety), delete, edit, anything over the network. Open: whether to ever allow writing a note back. Whether it ships with the free app is yours to decide.

Not proposing a roadmap list unless you want one; if you do, I would draft it from the sets you choose, each item marked Coming soon. Nothing above is called an "integration" on the site until it exists (B8 and the master apply).
