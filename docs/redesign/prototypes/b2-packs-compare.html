Notionists baseline

Licence
CC0 1.0 (dicebear.com/licenses, read today). Commercial use and modification allowed; no attribution.
Price
Free.
Poses
Busts, ten hand gestures, 25 bodies, 64 hair styles. No bodies below the chest.
Format
SVG, 12 to 13 named layers.
Motion
Move a hand, tilt a head, swap lips between variants.
Why a baseline
The owner still finds it goofy: wry half-lidded faces, long noses.

Micah (DiceBear) free

Maker
Micah Lanier, "Avatar Illustration System".
Licence
CC BY 4.0 (dicebear.com/licenses, read today). Commercial use and modification allowed. Attribution required: credit, a link to the licence, and a note that it was changed. One footer line would do it.
Price
Free.
Poses
Busts only, no hands at all. 8 hair styles, 5 eyes, 4 brows, 8 mouths, 3 noses, 3 shirts, glasses, beards. Skin, hair and clothing colours are free parameters, so full colour with skin tones works natively. Other mouths (frown, laughing, nervous, surprised) are goofy and were not used.
Format
SVG, about 15 groups, roughly 5 KB each.
Motion
Swap mouth or eye variants, nudge the whole head. No hands or arms to move.
Honest limits
Calm and friendly, which is the point, but plainer than Notionists: dot eyes and a simple smile on every face, and only about 8 hairstyles, so a cast looks related. Heavier outline-and-fill look, not the thin Notion line. No gestures, so the professor cannot point.

Bypeople "SVG Illustrations Pack" (welovegraphics sets) preview only

Maker
welovegraphics, sold by Bypeople (bypeople.com/svg-illustrations).
Licence
The page says only "Commercial use license" (read today). Modification, attribution and redistribution are not stated on the page, so they must be read before buying.
Price
The page body says $29; the buy button says 21.75 with no explanation. One-time. Nothing bought.
Poses
177 illustrations in 12 sets. The preview shows full scenes: people at laptops with headsets (Finofin), two people working on a diagram (Marker), a single woman (Cuterr). Whether the pack has a lecturer, students or an interview pair is not shown or stated.
Format
SVG, AI, Figma source and transparent PNG (2000px).
Motion
Unknown without the files. With Figma source a scene can be split into figure, desk and props, so Motion could move parts.
Honest limits
Closest to "outline-led with colour fills" and the people have natural proportions in the best sets, but the pack is business and website scenes, not a lecture, and the sets differ from each other in style. Some faces (Cuterr) look worried. Skin colour appears to be fixed per illustration.
