B1. Positioning sentence: three options (placeholder type)

A · recommended

Trilsy is a Mac app that records any call, lecture or interview and writes the notes. No bot joins.

What it is
A Mac app that records and writes notes.
Who for
Anyone in a conversation: call, lecture, interview.
Different
No bot. Stated last, so it is the thing the visitor leaves with.
Risk
Least distinctive of the three; it leans on the hero visual to carry personality.
B · mechanism first

Trilsy listens to your Mac and your microphone, then turns what was said into notes. Nothing joins the call.

What it is
Something on your Mac that listens and writes notes.
Who for
Left implicit: anyone with a conversation on a Mac.
Different
It explains how no bot is possible (it hears the Mac itself).
Risk
"Listens" can read as surveillance; says nothing about lectures or rooms.
C · open source first

Trilsy is the open-source notetaker for Mac: it records any conversation, writes the notes, and sends no bot into the room.

What it is
A notetaker, on Mac, open source.
Who for
Anyone; "any conversation" covers calls, lectures, interviews.
Different
Two differences at once: open source and no bot. Open source is checkable on GitHub; none of the other nine pages in the teardown leads with it.
Risk
"Open-source" narrows the first read toward developers, against the universal positioning.
