The characters are drawn on white with black outline and white trousers. On the dark page their white would glow, so in dark mode they sit on a paper-coloured card (a tile) rather than the page. Open decision for the owner, small.

Rules taken: the palette is the owner's character sheet (sampled from the image: mustard, terracotta, dusty blue, sage, skin tones, black outline), warm paper instead of the sheet's pure white so the page does not look clinical; terracotta and sage are fill colours and a deeper terracotta carries text so contrast stays above 4.5:1; gradients are not used anywhere; no teal (the earlier preview's teal was mine and is dropped). All values are placeholders until the owner agrees.
