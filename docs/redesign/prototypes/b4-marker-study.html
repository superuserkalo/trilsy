Three markers drawn from real highlighters (left: the real photograph each borrows from). Live, 600ms draw-in on the site's easing token.
1. Chisel band fixed SVG

Be in the conversation.
Trilsy writes the notes.

From the photocopy photo: a band of constant width that covers the x-height and a little below the baseline, not the whole line; ends cut at an angle where the chisel tip lands and lifts; a slight tilt off the baseline; translucent, so the letters stay fully dark; edges nearly straight with only a dry, slightly uneven roughness; a darker rim where the ink pools at each end.

2. Two passes, dry edge fixed SVG

Be in the conversation.
Trilsy writes the notes.

From the Stabilo photo: a first pass high on the line with a dry, feathered upper edge, and a second offset pass lower and starting later, so the overlap is visibly darker as real ink is; lengthwise fibre streaks, lighter threads inside the stroke; ends angled.

3. Quick stroke with a lifted tail fixed SVG

Be in the conversation.
Trilsy writes the notes.

From the "flesh and blood" mark in the photocopy photo: one fast pass that sits low, blunt where it starts with ink pooled at the start, tilting up so the end lifts and tapers to a tip that flicks just past the last letter. The most gestural of the three.
