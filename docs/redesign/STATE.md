- Both north stars break one of our hard rules. Wispr puts an eyebrow above the H1; Superwhisper's first viewport shows no product. Borrow their craft, not those two choices.

From 01b:

- **The photo effect is three things stacked:** a pinned card whose frame changes shape with scroll, a photograph that stays the same while the frame changes, and a 700ms noise-smear swap to a different photograph when a chapter changes. It needs one photograph that survives a wide crop and a portrait crop, plus one portrait photograph per extra chapter. This shapes the image requests in Phase B.
- **None of the three sites uses a smooth-scroll library.** Wispr uses GSAP ScrollTrigger; Superwhisper uses Motion; Aerivoice uses nothing. `position: sticky` in a tall wrapper reproduces every pin measured.
- **The good sections split the work between scroll and clock.** Scroll decides whether something is on stage and how it is framed; the product demo inside it plays in real time. Wispr's chapters, Aerivoice's laptop demo and Superwhisper's stage all work this way.
- **All three change or drop their scroll effects on phones.** Wispr rebuilds the section as stacked cards that play in view; Superwhisper removes its hero effect under 800px; Aerivoice keeps its sequence with a shorter track and smaller frames.
- **Wispr's chapter zone snaps** one chapter per scroll gesture. It is the part most likely to annoy and should be tested on a trackpad and a wheel before we copy the idea.
- **The melt is a fragment shader**, so on our site it belongs to Phase C and vgpu, with an instant swap as the fallback and under reduced motion.
- **Aerivoice's laptop frames are a generated video cut into 100 stills**, according to its own manifest.
