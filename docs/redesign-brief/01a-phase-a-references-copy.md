# Phase A, session 1 of 2: References and copy (read-only, no code)

Read `00-master.md` first. Run the skills preflight. Use a real browser (Playwright or browser-use) and the Mobbin MCP.

Phase A is split across two sessions so each study gets full depth. This session does A1 and A2 only. The behavior study (A3) is `01b-phase-a-behavior.md`, in a fresh session.

## A1. Reference teardown

1. Screenshot first viewport plus first scroll at 1440px and 390px for: superwhisper.com, wisprflow.ai (and its notetaker page), otter.ai, fireflies.ai, Granola, plus 3 more meeting-notes or dictation products. Also pull 8 to 10 best-in-class SaaS landing pages from Mobbin (hero, trust strip, how it works, use cases, FAQ, footer).
2. Save to `/docs/redesign/refs/`.
3. Write `/docs/redesign/00-teardown.md`. Per product: H1, subhead, primary CTA, what the hero visual *is* (real UI / video / illustration / abstract), where the trust strip sits and what is in it, section order, what motion exists and what it communicates, and **what a stranger understands after 5 seconds**.
4. End with "patterns that recur" and "patterns to deliberately avoid", with screenshot evidence.

## A2. Copy case study (as important as the visuals)

Study the Wispr Flow notetaker page's copy most deeply, plus Superwhisper, Otter, Fireflies, Granola. Write `/docs/redesign/copy-playbook.md` with direct quotes as evidence:
- how the H1 states the outcome in plain words, and its sentence structure;
- how the subhead adds who it is for and the proof or difference;
- the voice: person, tense, sentence length, use of "you", humor, what they never say;
- how section headings are phrased (verb-led? question? outcome?) and how body copy supports them in one or two lines;
- how they handle "does it join my call?", "is it private?", "what does it cost?", "does it work with my tools?";
- CTA wording and microcopy (buttons, helper text, empty states, FAQ answers);
- which claims are specific and which vague, and why the specific ones work.

End with 8 to 10 copy rules for Trilsy in our tone, and before/after rewrites of at least 5 current lines: the hero headline "Meeting notes, written by your Mac." and subhead; the strip "No bot in the call / Works with every app / Recordings stay on your Mac / Notes you can edit and keep"; the "Record / Transcribe / Notes" steps.

## Done when

- Every product listed in A1 has both viewports saved in `/docs/redesign/refs/` and a complete entry in the teardown, including the 5-second line.
- Every bullet in A2 is answered with at least one direct quote, and the playbook ends with the rules and the before/after rewrites.
